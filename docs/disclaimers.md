# Disclaimer framework

## Standard disclaimer (appended to every output)

> This document was produced by the Digital Work Systems Guidelines Prototype
> as **guidance**, not legal advice. It is intended to assist the user in
> structuring their approach under the *Work Health and Safety Amendment
> (Digital Work Systems) Act 2026* (NSW). The Act and any guidelines made
> under s 118A are the authoritative sources. Users should consult a lawyer
> or SafeWork NSW before taking regulatory action.
>
> The tool does not resolve interactions with other legal regimes (Privacy
> Act 1988 (Cth), PPIPA, HRIPA, Workplace Surveillance Act 2005 (NSW), Fair
> Work Act 2009 (Cth), legal professional privilege, commercial confidence,
> or intellectual property). Where the tool flags such an interaction, the
> user is responsible for obtaining specialist advice.

## LLM-assisted draft label

Where a section of an output has been drafted with LLM assistance, it is
prefixed with:

> **[AI-assisted draft — please review and edit before use.]**

## Scope limits

The v1 prototype is scoped to:

- s 118(1)(a1) notices (EPH pathway).
- PCBU responses to such notices.
- HSR/worker orientation to the same.
- Inspector proportionality checks on evidence requests.

It is **not** a tool for:

- Resolving disputes under Part 7 Division 6 or s 142.
- Full Part 9 investigation planning.
- Advice on s 19(3)(c1) duty compliance generally.
- Any output not reviewed by a human before use.
